## Description
Generates a red passenger car suitable for placement in parking lots and driveways.

## Components
- Car body: 4.5m × 1.8m × 1.4m, red painted metal
- Front windshield: 1.2m × 0.8m × 0.05m, transparent glass
- Rear windshield: 1.0m × 0.6m × 0.05m, transparent glass
- Side windows: 0.4m × 0.3m × 0.05m each, transparent glass
- Front wheels: 0.65m diameter × 0.2m width, black rubber with steel rims
- Rear wheels: 0.65m diameter × 0.2m width, black rubber with steel rims
- Front bumper: 1.8m × 0.3m × 0.2m, red painted plastic
- Rear bumper: 1.8m × 0.3m × 0.2m, red painted plastic

## Dimensions
4.5m × 1.8m × 1.4m

## Materials
matte_black, brushed_steel, glass

## Three.js Approach
Use BoxGeometry for main car body, windshields, and bumpers. Use CylinderGeometry for wheels rotated on side. Use PlaneGeometry for side windows. Apply red material to body and bumpers, transparent material to glass elements, black material to tires, and metallic material to wheel rims.
